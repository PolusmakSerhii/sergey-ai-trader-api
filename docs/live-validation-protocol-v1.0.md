## 1. FIXED PARAMETERS

Capital at planned start:
$2,000

Risk per trade:
0.5% of equity at entry

Starting reference:
$10 = 1R at $2,000 equity

For each trade:

plannedRiskUsd = 0.5% of equity at the moment of entry

plannedRiskUsd is frozen for that trade after entry.

actualR =
netActualPnL / plannedRiskUsd

netActualPnL includes:
- actual execution prices
- fees
- funding
- all realized parts of the position

Model R is stored separately.

Position limits:
- maximum 3 simultaneous positions
- maximum 2 positions in the same direction
- maximum 1 position per instrument
- maximum total initial open risk = 3R / 1.5% equity

Lot sizing:
- calculate position size from actual entry price to frozen Stop Loss
- round position size DOWN
- never round up to force an entry
- if minimum tradable lot produces initial risk > 1.25R:
  SKIPPED: LOT SIZE

## 2. SIGNAL SELECTION / AVAILABILITY

LIVE means manual real-money execution of eligible SM1M A+ signals.

SM1M does NOT automatically send exchange orders.

Take all eligible A+ signals while protocol limits permit.

Manual entry is allowed only when:

1. the A+ signal remains valid
2. current price remains inside the original frozen Entry Zone
3. trader is available for immediate execution
4. Stop Loss and TP1 can be placed immediately
5. position/risk limits are not violated

If price has left the frozen Entry Zone:

SKIPPED: MISSED ENTRY

If trader cannot immediately execute and place protection:

SKIPPED: UNAVAILABLE

If capacity/risk limits prevent entry:

SKIPPED: RISK/CAPACITY LIMIT

Every skipped eligible A+ must be recorded with its reason.

Because execution is manual, the real-money validation cohort represents
A+ signals available during the trader's actual availability windows.
It is not an unbiased 24/7 sample of every A+ generated by SM1M.

Do not hide or remove UNAVAILABLE signals from the journal.

## 3. EXECUTION

For every traded signal:

- position size is calculated from ACTUAL entry to FROZEN Stop Loss
- Stop Loss is placed on the exchange immediately after entry
- TP1 order for 50% is placed in advance at frozen TP1

After TP1:
- 50% is realized
- remaining 50% continues
- Stop is moved to break-even
- SM1M HOLD/CLOSE re-analysis continues

For CLOSE:
execute at the first practical opportunity.

Record:
- decisionAt
- actualCloseAt
- closeDelay

Frozen SL and TP1 are not recalculated because actual entry differs from
model entry.

Record deterioration/improvement versus model entry as execution
cost/benefit.

## 4. OVERRIDES

Do not:
- skip otherwise eligible A+ because of trader intuition
- take otherwise ineligible signals because of trader intuition

- close early outside the defined strategy

If manual discretion nevertheless changes a trade:

override = YES

The trade:
- remains in total real-money P&L
- is analyzed separately
- is excluded from the clean strategy-performance cohort

## 6. RISK STOPS

Daily stop:
-2R

Weekly stop:
-5R

When deciding whether another position may be opened, consider:

- realized actualR for the applicable period
- remaining initial risk of open positions
- risk of the proposed new position

Reaching daily/weekly stop prevents NEW real-money entries until the next
day/week.

Existing positions are not automatically closed solely because the
daily/weekly limit was reached.

Completed-trade counter does not reset.

Emergency stop:

cumulative actualR <= -10R

Result:
- stop the real-money experiment
- no new real-money entries
- continue only PAPER until review

Measurement/execution sanity check only.

and a separate statistical/risk review.
